# Gap To Growth Guide

The AI That Learns How You Learn

Adaptive personal AI tutor identifying knowledge gaps and tailoring dynamic learning paths.

The Problem

•Every student learns differently, but most educational systems follow the same approach for everyone.

•Students struggle to identify their weaknesses and knowledge blind spots.

•Educators spend hours preparing assessments, tracking progress, and providing individual feedback.

Pain Point: Inefficiency, lack of personalization, and high manual operational overhead.

Your Challenge

Build an AI-powered learning system that understands a student's knowledge gaps and automatically creates a personalized learning experience.

Your solution should aim to:

Assess a student's understanding of a selected topic.

Identify knowledge gaps and generate personalized explanations and exercises.

Automatically adapt the learning path based on student performance.

Track progress and provide actionable feedback
